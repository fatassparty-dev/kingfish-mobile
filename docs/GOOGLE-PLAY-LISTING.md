## Full description

KingFish Bets is a sports analytics and odds research platform built for
faster, clearer research across the sports calendar.

Compare game lines, scan player props, check recent form, review player
profiles, generate cheat sheets, use fantasy football tools, and ask KingFish
for plain-English analysis backed by KingFish data. KingFish gives you the
numbers, context, and best available prices so you can make your own decisions.

Inside KingFish Bets:

- Live game lines across supported sports
- Player props with recent-form and hit-rate context
- Best available odds across supported regulated U.S. sportsbooks
- MLB cheat sheets for quick prop research
- MLB and NFL Game Factors with venue, weather, and scoring context
- Fantasy football rankings, research, and roster tools
- Ask KingFish analysis with current KingFish context
- One KingFish account across Android, iPhone, and web

KingFish Bets does not accept wagers, hold funds, or operate as a sportsbook.
It is an analytics and information platform for users 18 and older where
permitted by law, subject to any higher age requirement in the user's
jurisdiction. All statistics, research signals, tools, and analysis are for
informational and entertainment purposes only. Gambling involves risk. Please
bet responsibly and within your means.

## Reviewer access note

KingFish Bets is an analytics and sports information app. It does not accept
wagers, process bets, or link users to place wagers.

Provide Google Play review with a non-admin test account that has KingFish Bets
Pro enabled. The account must not require MFA, an expiring code, location
approval, or a purchase. Reviewers can use it to inspect premium boards, Ask
KingFish, cheat sheets, fantasy tools, Account, Help, legal pages, subscription
management, and in-app account deletion.

Do not put the test password in this tracked file.
